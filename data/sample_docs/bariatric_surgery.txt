SAMPLE COVERAGE POLICY (synthetic, for testing only) — Bariatric Surgery

Section 1. Coverage Criteria
Bariatric surgery (Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy, or laparoscopic
adjustable gastric banding) is covered for beneficiaries with a Body Mass Index
(BMI) of 40 or greater, OR a BMI of 35 or greater with at least one obesity-related
comorbidity such as type 2 diabetes, hypertension, or obstructive sleep apnea.

Section 2. Exceptions
Coverage does NOT apply if the beneficiary has a documented history of
non-adherence to a physician-supervised weight management program within the
preceding 24 months, EXCEPT where the prior non-adherence was due to a
documented medical complication unrelated to the weight management program
itself, as defined in Section 4.2 of the general medical necessity policy
(see MED-NEC-GEN-01, not included in this sample set).

Section 3. Documentation Requirements
Claims require a psychological evaluation within 12 months of the surgical date,
a nutritional consultation, and at least 6 months of physician-supervised weight
management documentation preceding the surgery date.

Section 4. Regional Variation
Some MAC jurisdictions require an additional multidisciplinary team evaluation
prior to approval; confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements before submission.

Section 5. Effective Dates
This determination is effective as of the most recent policy revision and
supersedes all prior versions.
