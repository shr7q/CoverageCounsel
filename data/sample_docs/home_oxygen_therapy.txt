SAMPLE COVERAGE POLICY (synthetic, for testing only) — Home Oxygen Therapy

Section 1. Coverage Criteria
Home oxygen therapy is covered for beneficiaries with a documented arterial blood
gas PO2 of 55 mm Hg or less, or an oxygen saturation of 88% or less, measured at
rest, during exercise, or during sleep, using a device approved for that purpose.

Section 2. Conditional Coverage
For beneficiaries with a PO2 between 56 and 59 mm Hg, coverage requires at least
one of the following: dependent edema suggesting congestive heart failure,
pulmonary hypertension, or documented hematocrit greater than 56 percent.

Section 3. Exceptions
Coverage does not apply for beneficiaries whose hypoxemia is expected to resolve
within 30 days, such as post-surgical or acute illness related hypoxemia, unless
re-testing after 30 days confirms the qualifying threshold persists.

Section 4. Documentation Requirements
An initial certificate of medical necessity must be signed by the treating
physician and renewed at 12 months for beneficiaries whose initial test showed
borderline qualifying values.

Section 5. Effective Dates
This determination is effective as of the most recent policy revision and
supersedes all prior versions.
