SAMPLE COVERAGE POLICY (synthetic, for testing only) — Total Knee Arthroplasty

Section 1. Coverage Criteria
Total knee arthroplasty (TKA) is covered when the beneficiary has radiographically
confirmed severe osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or post-traumatic arthritis of
the knee, AND has failed at least 3 months of conservative management including
physical therapy, NSAIDs, and at least one intra-articular corticosteroid or
hyaluronic acid injection, AND has documented functional limitation affecting
activities of daily living.

Section 2. Exceptions
Coverage does NOT apply if the primary indication is isolated patellofemoral
arthritis without tricompartmental involvement, unless a partial knee arthroplasty
is being billed instead under a separate policy (see LCD-KNEE-002).

Section 3. Documentation Requirements
Claims must include imaging reports dated within 6 months of the surgical date,
a physical therapy completion note, and a physician attestation of failed
conservative treatment.

Section 4. Regional Variation
This policy is issued by MAC Jurisdiction J-15. Beneficiaries in other MAC
jurisdictions should consult the corresponding Local Coverage Determination, as
conservative treatment duration requirements range from 6 weeks to 3 months
depending on jurisdiction.

Section 5. Effective Dates
This determination is effective as of the most recent policy revision and
supersedes all prior versions. Always confirm the effective date against the
current CMS Medicare Coverage Database before relying on this determination.
